My journey started with Architecture, where I completed my Master's degree, and later led me to UX Design and Frontend Development. My background in Architecture has shaped the way I think about design and how people interact with the spaces around them. Today, I bring that same way of thinking into the digital world.

I enjoy creating designs that are simple, easy to use and look good. I like trying out different ideas, solving problems and paying attention to details. For me, even the smallest things can make a difference in how someone experiences a product.

What I love about UX Design and Frontend Development is that I get to work on both the design and the development side. I enjoy starting with an idea, working on how it should look and feel, and then seeing it come to life through code.

I'm always curious to learn new things, improve my skills and try different approaches. I also enjoy working with other people, sharing ideas and learning from each other. I believe the best results come when people work well together and enjoy what they do.
